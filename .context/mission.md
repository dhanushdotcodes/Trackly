# Mission Context

## Project Goal
To provide a high-performance, scalable, and intuitive task management platform that simplifies organizational complexity and enhances team collaboration.

## Design Philosophy
- **Cleanliness**: A premium, minimal UI that reduces cognitive load.
- **Speed**: Leveraging modern technologies (FastAPI, Bun, Next.js) for fast interactions.
- **Scalability**: A multi-tenant architecture that can handle growth from small teams to large enterprises.
- **Reliability**: Strong type safety and integration-first testing to ensure a stable platform.
